About Britebyte

Britebyte is a small computer repair service focused on clear communication and practical solutions. Instead of selling you a new device, we try to get more life out of the one you already own whenever it makes sense.

From slow startup times to strange pop-ups and error messages, we explain what is happening in plain language, then walk through the options before any work begins.

What you can expect

  • Simple explanations without heavy jargon
  • Clear pricing before repairs start
  • Recommendations that match how you actually use your computer
  • Respect for your time, your data, and your privacy
